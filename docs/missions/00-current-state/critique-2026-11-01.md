the owner's decisions on the open questions are OR1–OR8. Three items were fixed the same day (de4a439f); the rest are

## The owner's decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| **OR1** | **The second left rail: several kits, shared where they fit, and each mode's own controls besides.** A kit is a set of SVG controls for one kind of drawing: points (mark style, colour by, size by, labels, time window, details), areas (fill, outline, hatching, opacity, labels), lines (width by, arrows, labels), fields (scale, contours, opacity, look) and time (window, step). A sub-mode takes the kits its drawing needs, then its own: War & Conflict its arcs, deaths rings, war map and theatres; Earth Weather its colour (temperature or rain). |
| **OR2** | **A mark-style selector on every point layer: Icon, Dot, Pin, Spike.** Icon is an SVG by what the item is about; Pin a line and a flag, as Earthquake3D draws; Spike a bar whose height is the magnitude. Starcom drafts the Glyph Lexicon picks for every layer, for the owner to review in one pass. |
| **OR3** | **Colour by kind, everywhere.** One shared palette of what things are about (armed conflict, disaster, unrest, cyber, health, energy and the rest), used by every mode; a mode's own hue stays on its frame and badge only; severity shows by size and brightness, not hue. |
| **OR4** | **One time bar.** The Temporal Nav and the live playback bar merge: one scrubber with the Live pill, play and rate on it, and Eco's history scrubber folded in; Psi Watch's Timestream docks above it as now. |
| **OR5** | **Civil Unrest and Protest Surges: one layer, two looks.** Events as icons by kind (protest, riot, strike, crackdown, curfew), surges as rings sized by the week-on-week rise, each on or off from its rail. |
| **OR6** | **Psi Watch open to everyone now**, ahead of the 9 November key change; `?psi=off` hides it for a session. |
| **OR7** | **RLSH: Starcom's own sign-up.** A short form the person signs as a Nostr note, the age rule first (no key under 13, RS37–RS44); it goes to a review list, and only profiles approved into the list Starcom signs are shown, never at a person's place. |
| **OR8** | **Lifeboats: ask Mecha Jono for more services** that publish station data (the Netherlands' KNRM, Germany's DGzRS, France's SNSM, Norway's NSSR, US Coast Guard stations); until then the layer says it covers the UK and Ireland. The note's text goes to the owner first. |

### The second round (1 November, after the first fixes)

The owner caught a write-off in the reply on scale: "one true scale" was two. Sizes and the Moon's distance are drawn
1:1; every other distance 1:19.7. The planets' distances keep their true proportion to each other, but stand nearly
twenty times too close against their own sizes, so the Sun looks about 10.5° across from the Earth instead of 0.53°.
