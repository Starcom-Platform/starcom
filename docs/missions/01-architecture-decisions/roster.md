# Decision D-RS — The roster: every kind of work, alone and together

**Status:** decided 2026-10-16 (RS1–RS16) and 2026-10-17 (RS17–RS40), the owner's picks; CR3 done; steps 1–7 built 2026-10-17.

The owner's critique asked what comes after Missions (CR4), then that Starcom support every word,
"individually and as a collective" (CR5), with nothing hardcoded (CR6): "These are things that should be

## What is decided

Starcom provides each **kind**; people and Mecha Jono make every **instance**. No operation, adventure or
campaign is written into the app (CR6).

## The safety rules every kind keeps

From the brainstorm, each from a harm real location games and crowd investigations caused: nothing to do
while moving fast; public, lawful places only, with a way to have one removed; never leave people waiting
in danger (the weather feeds call an adventure off); quote the authority for each hazard, never a limit of
Starcom's own; never a person as the target or the answer; no live map of who is out (counts by coarse
cell, late); an age rule (open).

## Built, step 1: the shape (2026-10-17)
