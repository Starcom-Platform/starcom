**GV0.1, fills on the sphere (C26), 3 hours.**
- **What.** In `GeometryFactory.buildTerritoryPolygons`, fit every flat fill (`thickness === 0`) with
  `subdivideToSphere`, radius = its own vertices' radius (globe + lift).
- **The tolerance: an eighth of the lift.** The bake uses a quarter of its clearance, but runtime fills sit at 0.5
  units, just above the clouds (0.4). A triangle's interior can sag 4/3 of the tolerance, so a quarter would still
  dip under the clouds.
- **The cache.** Bump `GEO_PIPELINE_VERSION`.
- **What it covers.** War & Conflict, lit countries, Hero Watch regions, region marks, GDACS, Spheres,
  Organisations, Zones and the national fallback path.
- **Done when:**
  - a test that no runtime fill's `maxTriangleSag` reaches its lift;
  - vertices counted before and after for Russia, Canada, Brazil, the US and the whole War & Conflict map;
  - close-up shots.

**GV0.2, borders always drawn, 5 hours.** The causes were found in the code, not guessed:

| Fault | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Three rebuilds in 2.5 s near altitude 1.6 | A second level-of-detail downshifter in the hover loop (`useNationalTerritories3D.ts` L1144–1155): 45 frames over 38 ms drop a level. It is not gated by `buildsUnderWayRef`, so a rebuild's own slow frames trigger the next, and the watcher climbs back. | Ignore slow frames while a build or fade runs; keep a floor for 30 s after a downshift; let the quality governor own the step, as `QualityStep` does for everything else |
| The default view sits on a boundary | Altitude 2.5 is distance 350, exactly `t0`, so floating point picks the level | Move `t0` off the default view |
| Borders late on first load | The scene is read at render time (`Globe.tsx` L1762), so nothing starts until a re-render. Each new level re-fetches the whole topology file with `cache: 'no-cache'` (`NationalTerritoriesService` L481). Then a 500 ms fade. The first level is the device tier's maximum, and a tier-C device swaps straight away. | Build on the globe's ready event; fetch the topology once a session; seed the first level from the camera distance |
| Borders tinted over | Resource Zones' fills draw at 190, over the borders at 100, in one group with its routes and marks; three.js sorts by group first | Moved to D4, which rebuilds that layer anyway: its fills become a layer of their own under the borders |
| Hairlines | 10 layers still draw `THREE.Line`, one pixel, chords between points: War & Conflict's war-map edges and province rings, AI Watch, Hero Watch, Raver Watch, Missions, region marks, GDACS tracks, intel outlines, roster ops | `buildBorderLineBatches` (`LineSegments2`, one draw, pixel widths), densified to ≤1° along great circles |
