# Code of conduct

Starcom is built by people who disagree about a great deal and still work together. This code says what that
takes. It follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Argue with the work, never with the person. Say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Credit others' work, and say plainly when you got something wrong.
- Keep what others tell you in confidence.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, threats, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, body or background.
- Sexual attention or imagery anyone has not asked for.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission: their address, their name behind a key, their
  messages. This is also one of the EIN's refusals: no claims naming private individuals.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions), in the project's Discord, and anywhere someone acts
for the project in public.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers on the project's [Discord](https://discord.gg/Mea5v8pQmt), or privately to the Nostr key in
[`/.well-known/security.txt`](https://starcom.app/.well-known/security.txt). Reports are read by the maintainers, kept
in confidence, and answered.

## What happens

The maintainers decide, in proportion to the harm, among four steps:
1. a private correction;
2. a warning;
3. a time away from the project's spaces;
4. a permanent removal.

They say what was decided and why to the person reported. Whoever reported is told what was done.
